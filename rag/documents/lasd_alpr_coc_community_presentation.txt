ALPR Privacy Policy
LASD maintains a written ALPR Privacy Policy governing system use
Policy establishes rules for:
• Collection of ALPR data
• Use of ALPR technology
• Data retention and destruction
• Information sharing
• Oversight, auditing, and accountability
Policy requires compliance with all applicable federal, state, and 
county laws.

Legal and Policy Framework
• California Civil Code sections 1798.90.5 – 1798.90.55 (SB 34)
• Government Code sections 7282 – 7284.12 (SB 54)
• LASD Manual of Policy and Procedures – 5-09/550.00 
through 5-08/550.50 
• ALPR Privacy Policy – 5-09/550.05
• Field Operations Directive 09-004, ALPR System
• Other Department policies protecting privacy, data privacy, 
and civil liberties
Department policies available at LASD.org/Transparency

What Data is Collected
ALPR systems capture information associated with vehicles
observed in public locations:
• License plate number
• Date and time of capture
• Geographic location of capture
• Image of the vehicle and license plate
This information is stored in the Department’s ALPR database for 
authorized law enforcement use.
No personal identifying information is collected.

How ALPR Data is Used
ALPR data may be used for authorized public safety purposes, including:
• Criminal investigations
• Identification and location of wanted persons
• Crime analysis
• Recovery of stolen vehicles
• Investigatory leads in subsequent investigations
• Missing persons and lifesaving efforts
ALPR data may NOT be used for: 
• Personal or non-work-related purposes
• Civil immigration enforcement

Data Retention
• Under revised policy, ALPR data will be retained for two 
years
• After two years, records are archived and no longer 
visible to general users
• Data is permanently deleted after five years
• Exceptions may apply for ongoing investigations, 
prosecutions, or legal proceedings such as appeals

Information Sharing
• ALPR data may only be shared with other public agencies for 
legitimate law enforcement purposes
• Data sharing requires an inter-agency agreement
• Partner agencies must comply with applicable laws (SB 34 and SB 
54) and privacy protections
• ALPR data may not be sold or used for commercial purposes
• ALPR data may not be shared for civil immigration enforcement 
unless required by law or pursuant to a judicial warrant
There are no inter-agency agreements with any federal 
agencies

Oversight and Transparency
• All ALPR queries and system access are logged
• Internal audits are conducted at least annually
• Semi-annual reports will be provided to:
• Office of Inspector General
• Civilian Oversight Commission
• Board of Supervisors
• Summary reports will be published on the Department’s 
transparency website
